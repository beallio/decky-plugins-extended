Decky Extended Plugins

A custom Decky Loader store that merges the official Deckbrew catalog with community plugins that aren't in it, installed and updated the normal way. Copy a URL below and paste it into Decky under Settings → General → Store Channel → Custom. Use stable unless you want prereleases.
